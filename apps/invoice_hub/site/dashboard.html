    My vendor bills: received, approved, GL coded

    Each bill is followed through your own mailbox using your rules below: who sent the email, who it went to, and what its subject says. The bill is identified by its PDF filename (vendor, invoice number and BOL), so the same bill is recognized in the received, approved and GL coded emails. Only filenames are read, never file contents. Change how your mail is read in Settings (the gear icon).

      My inbox rules & search

      How your bills are recognized

      A rule tells the tracker how to spot one stage of a bill in your own mailbox: who sent it, who it went to, what its subject says, and (optionally) which folder. Folders can differ from person to person, so senders and subjects do most of the work. Only you see your rules.

                    These tell the tracker how to read your mailbox. Other people's settings do not change yours.

                      How your bills are recognized

                      Separate several options with commas (for example two approvers). A message matches if it fits any option. Leave every box in a stage empty to switch that stage off. The folder is optional; empty searches every folder.

                      Your PDF filenames

                      Use {vendor}, {invoice} and {bol}; everything else is literal text. {vendor} and {invoice} are required. For example, {vendor} {invoice} - BOL {bol} reads "Shoppa's WW12345- BOL 12346.pdf".
